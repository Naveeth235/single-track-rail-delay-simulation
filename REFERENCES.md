# References

The project data notes record these railway sources as consulted on 22 November 2026.

1. Sri Lanka Railways, “Station Details in Main Line.” Used to identify the sequence of the three modelled stations. [Station list](https://www.railway.gov.lk/web/index.php?option=com_content&view=article&id=166&lang=en)
2. Sri Lanka Railways, “Journey Starting Station at Main Line.” Used for station naming, including Peradeniya Junction. [Station information (PDF)](https://www.railway.gov.lk/web/images/pdf/Ticket_Price_Line/main_line.pdf)
3. IdeaBeam Transport, “Podi Menike – Express Train 1005,” Colombo Fort to Badulla. Scheduled departure and arrival times yield 21 minutes from Peradeniya Junction to Gampola and 25 minutes from Gampola to Nawalapitiya. [Train 1005 timetable](https://transport.ideabeam.com/train/details/1005)
4. Amestri, “Podi Manike, A Fun Train Ride Through the Mountains of Sri Lanka (Colombo to Badulla),” down-train timetable. Scheduled times yield 27 minutes from Nawalapitiya to Gampola and 18 minutes from Gampola to Peradeniya Junction. [Return timetable](https://amestri.com/podi-manike-a-fun-train-ride-through-the-mountains-of-sri-lanka-colombo-to-badulla/)

## How the sources are used

The segment durations are historical schedule proxies and include intermediate stops within each modelled segment. The three-service timetable, its 10:00 crossing, the imposed disruption and the random-input scenarios were constructed for the experiment. The cited schedules supply timing inputs only.
